Game menu

Touch
Left stick moves, drag the right side to look. Hold Break to mine or fight. Place sets a block, opens tables and furnaces, or eats. Double-tap Jump to fly in Creative.
Mouse
WASD move, Space jump (double-tap to fly in Creative, Shift to descend), hold left click to mine or fight, right click to place, wheel or 1-9 picks a slot, E opens the bag, T opens chat on LAN.
